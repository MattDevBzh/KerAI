## Prompt 1

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished product concept by EDITING the provided Feature Studio dashboard screenshot. Reference image 1 is the screenshot to redesign: preserve its identity, forest-green interface palette, off-white typography, restrained rounded borders and clean readable French UI. Replace the dashboard cards with a new "Le QG" page. Reference images 2 and 3 supply the exact character design for the developer and tester; faithfully keep their faces, clothing, distinctive accessories and charming chunky pixel-art rendering, adapt their pose naturally to their workstation. This is a concept mockup, not a screenshot of implemented software.
Format: a wide landscape desktop-app screenshot, around 16:10, entire interface visible, no laptop or device frame, no external margin. Top header reads "Feature Studio · Le QG", smaller eyebrow "MAQUETTE". Small navigation tabs "Sessions", "QG", "Discussion", with QG selected. Below the heading: "L’équipe au travail". Two compact status chips: "2 agents actifs" and "1 validation attendue".
Main composition: 78 percent of the content is a large, lovingly detailed but uncluttered isometric pixel-art office dollhouse with a single readable floor plan. Honey-colored wood flooring, dark forest-green walls, warm lamps, plants, little bookshelves, cables and a small coffee corner. This is a professional software team inhabiting a playful miniature office. Four clearly separated work zones: upper left an architect with glasses standing by a whiteboard with a simple diagram; center left the developer from reference 2, green hoodie and orange headphones, typing at a dual-screen desk; lower right the detective tester from reference 3 at a diagnostic workstation with a magnifying glass, bug checklist and green/red test lamps; upper right a reviewer quietly reading a document beside a bookcase. Pixel-art people remain distinct and large enough to recognize. No duplicated characters, no generic robot mascots.
Use elegant flat UI name tags next to each zone, not perspective text burned into furniture: "Conception" with "Disponible"; "Développement" with green "En cours"; "Tests" with green "En cours"; "Relecture" with amber "Attend ton avis". Use separate activity symbols, not color alone. A subtle glowing path visually connects these four work areas. The developer and tester are visibly working, the reviewer has a small amber notification bubble. Keep the pixel-art world crisp and blocky, not blurry, voxel or realistic 3D; the surrounding UI uses modern sharp sans-serif lettering and spacious padding.
A narrow right sidebar titled "À ton tour" has one amber accented card with "Recherche de tickets", "Plan à valider", and a clear button "Ouvrir la session". Below, a small "L’équipe" list shows the four roles with small portraits and their statuses. A quiet footer reads "Clique sur un agent pour voir son travail."
All text French and legible, strong typography hierarchy, few large text blocks, no filler paragraphs, no invented charts, no fake percentages. Main focus is the attractive inhabitable office and clear mapping between agent, place and true work state. Make it genuinely usable as a product design, not a fantasy promotional poster.
