Exact consistent scale, head size, colors, feet baseline and center alignment across all cells, at least 15 percent transparent margin inside every cell. Small game sprite style with clean hard pixel edges, warm golden orange fur and cream chest, no accessories, no clothing. Transparent background everywhere between and around sprites. No floor, no shadows on background, NO grid lines, no cell borders, no letters, no labels, no text, no logos, no extra cats in any cell. Six cats exactly arranged in the specified equal 3x2 grid.
